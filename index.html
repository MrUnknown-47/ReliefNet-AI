🚨 The Problem

Disaster response today is slow, reactive, and unstructured. Critical decisions are made without real-time intelligence, causing delays, poor coordination, and loss of lives.

💡 Our Solution

ReliefNet AI is an intelligent disaster response system that:

  • Analyzes crisis reports using AI
  • Predicts disaster severity
  • Matches the best volunteers
  • Visualizes everything on a real-time map

🧠 AI Engine (Core Innovation)

NLP Analyzer

Extracts crisis type, urgency, and needs from raw text reports.

Prediction Engine

Forecasts disaster risk using rainfall, population, and history.

Volunteer Matching

Matches volunteers based on distance and skill relevance.

Explainable AI

Provides reasoning behind predictions for trust and transparency.
